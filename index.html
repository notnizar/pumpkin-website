Who we are

A small team with a big appetite for brands that mean something.

Pumpkin Group started in Amman in 2020 with a simple idea: a brand should feel the same on a billboard on Mecca Street as it does in a 15-second reel. Most agencies split that work across departments. We keep it in one room.

Strategists, designers, writers and media planners work on every account together, so the thinking that shapes a logo also shapes the launch campaign, the Instagram grid and the landing page.

Strategy before pixels

Every engagement starts with the business question, not the deliverable. We find out what needs to change before we decide what to make.

Bilingual by default

Arabic and English are designed together from day one, so nothing reads like a translation.

Measured, not guessed

We report on reach, engagement, cost per result and sales lift. If a channel isn't working, we say so and move the budget.

How we work

From first call to launch in four moves.

No black box. You see the thinking at every step, and you always know what happens next.

01

Discover

A working session with your team, a look at your market and competitors, and a clear statement of the problem we're solving. Usually one to two weeks.

02

Define

Positioning, audiences, message hierarchy and a creative brief that everyone signs. This is where most of the hard decisions get made.

03

Design

Identity, campaign concepts and digital experiences, presented as finished directions rather than mood boards. Two rounds of refinement are built in.

04

Deliver & grow

Production, media launch and a monthly performance review. We stay on as your marketing team for as long as you need us.
